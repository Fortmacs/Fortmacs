2026-10-31 — One word per meaning (`fortran-first-verbs`): the owner's decisions V1 and V2 applied.  V1: execute is the only verb for running something, user-visible names included: `call_interactively`, `call_last_kbd_macro`, the `kmacro_call_*` commands and `kmacro_call_repeat_key`, `run_at_time`, `run_with_timer`, `run_with_idle_timer`, `run_mode_hooks`, `safe_run_hooks`, `call_process*`, `call_shell_region` and `comint_run` become `execute_*` (`kmacro_execute_macro`, `comint_execute`), the type-bound and pointer `%run`/`%run_safe` of commands, major modes, hooks, yank handlers and VC actions become `%execute`/`%execute_safe` (config API: `call commands%yank%execute(status)`), emacs_fm's `run` is `execute_editor`, and `execute_charset` (a membership test) is `match_charset`; 79 per-name lines of tools/fortran-names, no `term`, so the nouns `runs`, `run_prop`, `excluded_run` stay.  The procedure-pointer wrappers all do more than dispatch (a default, a status, a lookup or saved state), so they are kept under execute_ names; the unused `call_diff_function` is removed.  V2: synonyms for one meaning in internal helpers are merged (fetch into get, new into make, release into free, the forgetting of local values into kill_local, set_local_X into X_set_local, `%get` into `%current`, put into set, finish/save/restore into begin/end, prev into previous, Y_from_X into X_to_Y, interface suffixes into _proc/_predicate, no _fn/_fun suffixes, push and append of one element into add), and the logical `test_completion`, `test_undefined` and `verify_visited_file_modtime` become `is_exact_completion`, `is_undefined_binding` and `is_visited_file_modtime_current`; 143 more lines, with bare `push`/`append`/`put` helpers renamed in code only.  tools/intrinsics.awk gains the verb rule (a new `run`, `run_*` or `call_*` procedure, pointer or binding fails), with its fixture; CLAUDE.md states the rule, and PORTING.md gains "One word per meaning", a design decision and two decision-guide rows.
