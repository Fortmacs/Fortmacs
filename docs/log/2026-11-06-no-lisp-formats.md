2026-11-06 — No Emacs-style %-directive formats (`no-lisp-formats`, owner: "%s and other Lisp-isms are never used.  Only Fortran-isms.").  CLAUDE.md's Intrinsics bullet and PORTING.md's section, renamed "Text from a format" (was "Numbers to text"), now say that any text built from a format in the source, numbers or strings, is one internal `write` with edit descriptors or a concatenation of strings, and that `format`, `format-message`, `%s`/`%d`/`%S` and `format_arguments_type` are never used.  tools/intrinsics.awk has a percent-format rule (a call of `format_text`, `styled_format` or `format_message`, a use of `format_arguments_type`), with a fixture case.  The cleanup: error_mod's `format_message`, which only curved quotes, is `substitute_quotes` (help.el:substitute-quotes, decided by rule; a name entry in tools/fortran-names.d renames branches written before it, such as #305); display_engine_mod's `message` lost its format ARGUMENTS and STATUS (only a test used them); editing_functions_mod's `styled_format` and its MESSAGE flag are gone (format-message is Not applicable), `format_text` stays as the one port; ibuffer_mod's `format_message_of` and message_mod's `format_message_quote` wrappers are gone; the hand-made %s substitutions are gone: dired's `format_s` (`dired-mark-read-string` and `dired-mark-read-file-name` take PROMPT_BEGINNING and PROMPT_END around the marked files), `format_d` (`dired-do-kill-lines`' FMT is QUIET), vc's `substitute_keys` (`vc-start-logentry` concatenates) and `set-transient-map`'s %k (MESSAGE_BEGINNING and MESSAGE_END); tab_line_mod's local `format_text` is `tab_line_format_value`.  Left for the owner (baselined): the format strings users give at run time, `keyboard_macro_counter_format` (kmacro-counter-format, C-x C-k C-f) and the FORMAT of `rectangle_number_lines` (C-u C-x r N), which still go through `format_text`.  Not touched: mode-line %-constructs and format-time-string.  PR #305 (port-warnings) follows once its question on #261 is answered.
