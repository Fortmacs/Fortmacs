2026-11-06 — Rmail MIME display (`port-rmailmm`).  New modules: rmail_mime_mod (rmailmm.el): with `rmail_enable_mime` (the default) a message with a MIME-Version field is parsed into MIME entities (records of a registry, their index the text property `rmail_mime_entity`) and shown in Rmail's view buffer: text parts decoded by their transfer encoding (base64, quoted-printable) and charset, multipart/alternative showing the best text part (`rmail_mime_prefer_html`), message/rfc822 with its header, the other parts as tag lines whose buttons show or hide the part (RET, TAB and S-TAB in Rmail mode) and save an attachment; v (`toggle_rmail_mime`) toggles the raw message, and with `rmail_enable_mime` false shows a decoded copy in "*RMAIL*" (RMIME mode) through `rmail_mime_media_type_handlers`; HTML parts render through lynx when it is installed; searching a MIME message searches its decoded parts.  rfc2231_mod (rfc2231.el in part: parsing Content-Type and Content-Disposition values with continued and encoded parameters) and ietf_drums_mod (ietf-drums.el in part: removing comments and whitespace), which it needs; both scan the value as a string with the rules of upstream's syntax tables.  rmail_mod calls `rmail_show_mime_function` and `rmail_search_mime_message_function` where upstream does, decodes RFC 2047 words in the header of other messages (rfc2047_mod, #297), binds v, and `copy_rmail_headers` takes the view buffer.  Deferred (status files): images, shr, `rmail-mime-save-action`, forwarding and resending MIME messages, rfc2231-encode-string, the rest of ietf-drums.el.  Tests: test_rmail_mime_mod (rmailmm-tests.el, and own tests in Rmail), test_rfc2231_mod (own), test_ietf_drums_mod (ietf-drums-tests.el's cases of the two ported functions).
