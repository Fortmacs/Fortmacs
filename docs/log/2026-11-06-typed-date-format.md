2026-11-06 — Dates as a typed Fortran date format (`typed-date-format`, owner, #272).  The owner chose option (c3) of panel question 20: a date and time format is a `date_format_type`, a Fortran format with edit descriptors and the DATE_ parts it writes, in source and settings alike, written by `format_date`.  time_functions_mod gains the type (with a generic constructor taking any number of parts), 39 part constants for the values and C-locale names of strftime's conversions (and the sign, hours, minutes and seconds of the zone offset), `MAIL_DATE` for the Date field shared by message.el, sendmail.el and smtpmail.el, and `format_date`, which writes each part alone with its own descriptor and then the whole format, its data edit descriptors turned into A, in one `write`, so the text is exactly the format's; a mismatch between descriptors and parts, a bad format, a format reversion, an unlimited repeat or a new record is an error through the status.  `format_time_string` and its port of nstrftime are gone (format-time-string is not applicable); the ten callers in seven modules use date formats giving the same text, tested against GNU date's output for their strftime strings.  Statuses now reach every caller: `add_log_time_procedure`, `mail_rfc822_date`, `make_message_date`, `make_message_expires`, `make_message_expires_date` and `is_log_edit_changelog_ours` take one.  No setting held a strftime string yet.  The timefns ERT tests keep their checks with date formats, except %-N, %-6N and %-:::z, whose digits depend on the value; iso8601's zone round trip keeps %z and %:z.  CLAUDE.md and PORTING.md ("Dates to text") record the rule.
