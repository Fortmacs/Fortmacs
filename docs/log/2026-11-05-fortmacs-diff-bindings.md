2026-11-05 — The Ediff family as type-bound procedures (`fortmacs-diff-bindings`, panel 14, the second long-name PR; built on #292).  Every helper that acts on one Ediff session is a type-bound procedure of `fortmacs_diff_session_type`, called on the session's entry of `fortmacs_diff_sessions` by its index, `call fortmacs_diff_sessions(session_index)%quit(.false., status)`, as panel 13's `check_region_skip` already was; every helper that acts on one session group is one of `fortmacs_diff_session_group_type`, `fortmacs_diff_session_groups(group_index)%get_session_at(position)`.  54 session procedures and 26 group procedures became bindings, verb first and spelled out, "difference" where upstream's "diff" is a difference region and "diff" where it is the program or its output: `select_difference`, `unselect_and_select_difference`, `copy_difference`, `quit`, `recenter`, `save_and_delete_merge`, `compute_custom_diffs`, `set_difference_state`, `set_all_difference_states`, `merge_differences`, `combine_differences`, `check_region_changed`, `make_difference_overlays`, `set_up_difference_regions`, `make_diff2_buffer`, `paint_background_regions`, `refresh_mode_lines`, `set_help_message`; `set_up_meta_map`, `update_markers`, `next_item`, `draw_directory_diffs`, `operate_on_marked_sessions`, ...  The old free procedures are gone, with no aliases; commands keep their names.

Placement: the session type is in fortmacs_diff_settings_mod (ediff-init.el), and the bodies of its bindings must be there or in its submodules, while they use the modules that use it.  So the session procedures of each upstream file moved into a new submodule of fortmacs_diff_settings_mod named for it: fortmacs_diff_settings_diff_mod (ediff-diff.el, with the diff3 parsing helpers `get_fortmacs_diff_diff3_group`, `matched_number` and `get_line_position`, used only there), fortmacs_diff_settings_merging_mod (ediff-merg.el, with `check_region_skip`; fortmacs_diff_settings_skip_mod is gone), fortmacs_diff_settings_utilities_mod (ediff-util.el, with the helpers only they use), fortmacs_diff_settings_window_mod and fortmacs_diff_settings_help_mod.  fortmacs_diff_utilities_session_mod stays a submodule of fortmacs_diff_utilities_mod with the commands; the three helpers both sides use (`enter_fortmacs_diff_buffer`, `select_window_begin`, `select_window_end`) are now declared public in fortmacs_diff_utilities_mod, with a comment saying why.  The diff record type moved to fortmacs_diff_settings_mod (it is in the bindings' interfaces), and the FLAG values of `unselect_and_select_difference` are `FORTMACS_DIFF_SELECT_ONLY` and `FORTMACS_DIFF_UNSELECT_ONLY` there.  The group type and its procedures share fortmacs_diff_multiple_mod, so its bindings are all private there.

Hazard R (the registry can grow, and move, while a bound procedure runs: a new session, or a hook running user code): a bound subroutine finds its entry again on entry, by its serial number (`findloc(fortmacs_diff_sessions%serial, self%serial, dim=1)`) or for a group by its buffer (`fortmacs_diff_group_index(self%buffer)`), and works through the registry from then on, never through SELF; SELF has `intent(inout), target`.  A function has no side effects and reads SELF.  PORTING.md has the rule (Conventions, "Type-bound procedures on registry objects") and the Hazards checklist an item.

Left free: `set_up_fortmacs_diff_keymap` (it binds keys to the private command procedures of fortmacs_diff_utilities_session_mod, which a submodule of fortmacs_diff_settings_mod cannot reach); the registry procedures that find, make or clear an entry (`fortmacs_diff_session_index`, `make_fortmacs_diff_session_entry`, `drop_fortmacs_diff_session`, `check_fortmacs_diff_control_buffer`, `fortmacs_diff_group_index`, `make_group_entry`, `check_meta_buffer`), which act on the registry, not on one object.

Names: the session index is `session_index` and the group index `group_index` everywhere in the Ediff modules and tests (`k`, `s` and `g` where they stood for one; `parent_index` and `registry_index` where a procedure has two); a dummy `n` of a binding is `difference` or `session_number`.  `recenter` is bound to `recenter_session`, since window_mod's `recenter` is imported in the same submodule.  tools/fortran-names.d: the step 48c and 48d entries of the procedures that are now bindings name the bindings (fixed in place, as #292 did for `check_region_skip`).  tools/lisp-candidates.sh against #292's branch: "vector" and "form" in moved docstrings, kept (upstream's vector is described; "form" is plain English).  Unused imports of the touched Ediff files and tests were dropped.

Tests: the Ediff suites call the bindings (`quit`, `get_difference_position`, `merge_differences`, `combine_differences`, `save_and_delete_merge`) and pass unchanged otherwise; tools/check.sh --fix passes.
