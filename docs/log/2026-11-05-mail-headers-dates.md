2026-11-05 — Mail header encoding and date parsing (`mail-headers-dates`), which the Message (#293) and Rmail (#295) pull requests wait for.  New modules: rfc2047_mod (rfc2047.el with mail-prsvr.el's variables; mail-parse.el's aliases such as mail-decode-encoded-word-string and mail-encode-encoded-word-buffer collapse to the rfc2047 names): decoding B and Q encoded words, loose Q words, incomplete text across words, charsets through the coding systems, address decoding, the optional quoting of decoded words with special characters; encoding addresses (quoted strings, comments as unstructured text, bogus delimiters), unstructured fields and whole headers by `rfc2047_header_encodings`, with the 76-column encoded-word lines of rfc2047-encode-1; folding and unfolding.  quoted_printable_mod (qp.el, which rfc2047.el uses for Q words): decoding and encoding regions and strings, two commands.  mime_utilities_mod (mm-util.el in part): the coding system of a MIME charset (overrides, synonyms, coding-system-from-name, the coding systems' MIME charsets) and the MIME charset of a region (find-coding-systems sorted by `mime_coding_system_priorities`); coding-system-from-name and canonicalize-coding-system-name come into multilingual_environment_mod exactly as #295 adds them, so the two merge cleanly.  iso8601_mod (iso8601.el, all of it), parse_time_mod (parse-time.el: the rules are code, the month, weekday and zone tables records), and time_date_mod now holds all of time-date.el: decoded times whose components may be unknown (`partial_decoded_time_type`, unallocated for upstream's nil, its constructor make-decoded-time), `decoded_time_add`, `set_decoded_time_defaults`, `format_seconds`, the day arithmetic, and `date_to_time` with its siblings in the submodule time_date_parse_mod (parse_time_mod sits above time_date_mod).  mail_utilities_mod ports `mail_mbox_from`.  Deferred (status files): message-posting-charset (Gnus only), rfc2047-encode-parameter (rfc2045.el), mm-util.el's fallback for text no MIME charset covers and HACK-CHARSETS, mm-use-ultra-safe-encoding, fractions and numbers beyond int64.
