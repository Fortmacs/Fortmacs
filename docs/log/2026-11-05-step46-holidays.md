2026-11-05 — Step 46b, the holidays (`step46-holidays`).  New holidays_mod (holidays.el) with the submodules cal_julian_mod and cal_islam_mod (the conversions of cal-julian.el and cal-islam.el, short and pure arithmetic, which the Christian and Islamic holidays need).  A holiday list is a `holidays_type` of `holiday_entry_type` records written with keyword constructors and added with `%append` (`%clear` empties one): `holiday_entry_type(kind=HOLIDAY_ENTRY_FIXED, month=7, day=14, string="Bastille Day")`; the kinds are fixed, float, expression (holiday-sexp: a procedure pointer giving the date in a year, R6-37), Easter, Advent, Greek Orthodox Easter, Julian, Islamic, and a procedure computing a list of holidays (holiday-islamic-new-year, and the default lists' conditional groups behind `calendar_christian_all_holidays_flag` and `calendar_islamic_all_holidays_flag`).  Ported: the general, local, other, Christian and Islamic lists, `calendar_holidays` (made from them when first used, as upstream loads holidays.el after the init file), `calendar_holiday_list` (a stable sort by date, later entries first on a date, as upstream), M-x holidays, M-x list_holidays (and holiday_list) with its choices, `calendar_check_holidays`, `holiday_in_range`, and the calendar's a, h and x (u unmarks); `calendar_mark_holidays_flag` and `calendar_view_holidays_initially_flag` work through two procedure pointers calendar_mod calls (holidays_mod uses calendar_mod).  calendar_mod gained `calendar_in_read_only_buffer`, `calendar_set_mode_line` (the mode line shows `%-` as dashes and `%%` as `%`), `is_calendar_month_visible`, `is_calendar_nongregorian_date_visible` and `set_calendar_display`/`restore_calendar_display` (upstream's let of displayed-month, displayed-year and calendar-total-months).  Deferred: the Hebrew, Bahá'í, Chinese and solar holidays (cal-hebrew.el is long; the others need solar.el and lunar.el), the warning for a failing entry, `calendar_holiday_marker`, the mouse.  Tests: test_holidays_mod ports holidays-tests.el's two tests and checks list_holidays for 2026 and (all holidays) 2024 against text GNU Emacs made from the 31.1 sources.  A fresh build needed `rm -rf build/gfortran_*`: fpm left etags_mod.mod stale against a recompiled ansi_color_mod.mod (a vtype mismatch in docstrings_mod) and did not rebuild libfortmacs.a.
