2026-11-03 — Step 45a: Winner mode and windmove (`step45a-winner-windmove`).  New modules winner_mod (winner.el) and windmove_mod (windmove.el), and the window infrastructure they need.  window_mod: `window_in_direction` replaces `window_in_direction_below` (all four directions as DIRECTION_ABOVE, DIRECTION_BELOW, DIRECTION_LEFT and DIRECTION_RIGHT, with SIGN, WRAP and MINIBUF as optional arguments; display-buffer-below-selected calls it with DIRECTION_BELOW), `split_window` takes an optional BEFORE (SIDE `above` or `left`, through `split_window_internal`), window configurations record the frame's size and are compared (`is_window_configuration_equal`, window.c's compare_window_configurations) and copied (`copy_window_configuration`), and the new submodule window_change_mod runs `window_configuration_change_hook` from redisplay (`execute_window_change_functions`, the configuration part of run_window_change_functions, with window_change_record; window_type records each window's old buffer, change stamp and sizes).  hooks_mod: `execute_local` and `execute_default` (run_funs on a buffer-local or the default value).  Winner mode keeps one ring for the one frame, an array of configurations newest first; its keys are bound the first time the mode runs, as loading winner.el binds them; `winner_repeat_map` gives winner_undo and winner_redo their repeat map.  Windmove: the four motion commands, `windmove_mode` (on) with its map, `windmove_default_keybindings` and `windmove_delete_default_keybindings` with modifiers as optional strings, the delete commands, wrapping and creating windows.  Deferred: windmove's display and swap-states commands, the :set options, the `no-other-window` override, the obsolete functions; Winner's :set after loading, `select-enable-primary`, the minibuffer's height.  Tests: test_winner_mod (ports winner-dont-bind-my-keys, its first check; own tests of undo and redo, boring buffers, comparing configurations and the change hook), test_windmove_mod (own).
