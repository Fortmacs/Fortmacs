2026-11-04 — Shared stems (`shared-stems`): the owner's decision on issue #261 (option 1) applied.  A stem several options share must be the common start of their names; otherwise each option gets its own family of named constants with its full name as prefix, holding only the states that option takes, and code that passes a state from one option to another maps the constants explicitly.  `DABBREV_*` (the dabbrev_* options), `SH_PLUS` and its siblings (the sh_indent_* options), `DIRED_RECURSIVE_*` and `ANSI_COLOR_FILTER` already follow it and stay.  Renamed: `QUICK_KEYS_INDENT`/`_CLOSE` are `SGML_QUICK_KEYS_INDENT`/`_CLOSE` and `HTML_QUICK_KEYS_INDENT`/`_CLOSE`, `QUICK_KEYS_DEFAULT` is `HTML_QUICK_KEYS_DEFAULT` (sgml_quick_keys no longer lists it); `FINAL_NEWLINE_VISIT`/`_VISIT_SAVE`/`_ASK` are `REQUIRE_FINAL_NEWLINE_*` and `MODE_REQUIRE_FINAL_NEWLINE_*` (log_edit_require_final_newline already had its own `LOG_EDIT_REQUIRE_FINAL_NEWLINE_ASK`); the search of src for other shared families found `COMINT_AUTOEXPAND_INPUT`/`_HISTORY`, shared by comint_input_autoexpand and shell_input_autoexpand and not even the start of either name, now `COMINT_INPUT_AUTOEXPAND_*` and `SHELL_INPUT_AUTOEXPAND_*`.  The copies across options map the constants: text and prog modes call files_mod's new `set_require_final_newline_from_mode`, Shell mode maps shell_input_autoexpand into comint_input_autoexpand, and sgml_mode_mod's `set_quick_keys` maps sgml_quick_keys into html_quick_keys; data_buffer_mod's final-newline value readers take the option's constants.  html_quick_keys keeps a `_CLOSE` state (not in its :type) because upstream copies sgml-quick-keys's `close` into it.  CLAUDE.md, PORTING.md (the named-constant rule, "Choice settings", the R2-Q15 and R4-9 rows, a Before/After row) and tools/fortran-names (the renames, with keep entries for the new names that contain the old ones) follow; new tests: files' mode-require-final-newline and shell's shell-input-autoexpand.
