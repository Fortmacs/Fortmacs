2026-11-02 — Step 42e, timefns.c (`step42e-timefns`).  The new timefns_mod gives Lisp's time values their Fortran form, R6-3's `time_type` (`seconds` plus `count`/`count_rate`), and ports what timefns.c does with them: `current-time`, `current-cpu-time`, `time-add`, `time-subtract`, `time-less-p`, `time-equal-p`, `float-time`, `time-convert`, `decode-time`, `encode-time`, `format-time-string`, `current-time-string`, `current-time-zone` and `set-time-zone-rule`.  Arithmetic is exact for any int64 count rate: products go through a 128-bit integer kind, a sum takes the least common multiple of the rates reduced as upstream reduces it, and `float-time` rounds the quotient once, ties to even, as `frac_to_double` does (the conversion of the rounded quotient must drop its low digit, as mpz_get_d truncates, or an odd quotient rounds twice).  The calendar comes from the C library under a TZ rule set for the call (localtime_r, mktime, tzset bound in src/sys; TZ is set for the C library only, as upstream's set-time-zone-rule leaves `process-environment` alone), and `format-time-string` is a Fortran port of Gnulib's strftime in the C locale: all 16 ERT tests of timefns-tests.el pass, the padding tests included.  A time zone is a TZ string ("UTC0" for t); the integer and (OFFSET ABBR) forms and `wall` wait for decisions (questions), and an integer zone already works in `encode-time` through `decoded_time_type%zone`.  A real converted to a time value takes the least power of 2 that holds it, not upstream's 2**(digits - exponent): the value is the same, and a sum with a nanosecond clock time then fits int64.  The repository's ad-hoc times now use it: the buffer's visited-file modtime (with `visited-file-modtime`, `set-visited-file-modtime`'s TIME-FLAG and the undo list's first-change entries, which compare time values as simple.el does), `buffer-display-time` (ibuffer's recency column, sort and old-buffer marking), `set-file-times`'s TIMESTAMP, editfns_mod's `current_time_string` (moved, and its weekday no longer computed by hand), compile.el's start and end lines, dired-aux.el's touch default, add-log.el's ISO 8601 date, the generation list and the progress reporter's `float-time`; sysdep_mod's `emacs_time`, `emacs_localtime` and `emacs_localtime_wday` are gone.  Times kept elsewhere in their own form (file_stat_type's, info's, savehist's, bookmark's, vc's, the timers' `system_clock` counts) are listed in the row.
