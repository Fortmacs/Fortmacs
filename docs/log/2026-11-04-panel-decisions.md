The owner asked for a decision panel: a question no rule covers, with a
recommended option, first goes to three independent agents that see the
options and the project rules but not Claude's pick. If all three pick the
recommended option, it is decided ("panel"), recorded like an owner decision
and reported. Otherwise it is added to the "Owner questions" issue (#261)
with each agent's pick and reason, and asked when the owner is present.
