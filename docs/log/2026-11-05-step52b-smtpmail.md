2026-11-05 — Step 52b: sending mail over SMTP (`step52b-smtpmail`).  New module smtpmail_mod (smtpmail.el): `send_mail_with_smtp` (smtpmail-send-it, the owner's name) completes the header (From in the angles, parens and plain styles, Message-Id from message.el's message-make-message-id, Date, the MIME header, Fcc), takes the recipients and removes Bcc, then talks to `smtpmail_smtp_server` (greeting, EHLO or HELO, the extensions, AUTH with `smtpmail_auth_supported`: CRAM-MD5 through fns_mod's MD5 as rfc2104.el's HMAC, PLAIN, LOGIN and XOAUTH2, MAIL FROM with SIZE, RCPT TO, DATA with dot-stuffing, QUIT; retries after 4xx, credentials after 530 or 550-554), keeping the dialogue in "*trace of SMTP session to HOST*" with the default sentinel's messages; with `smtpmail_queue_mail` it queues in `smtpmail_queue_dir`, the message's variables in a namelist file FILE.nml beside it (the saved-state rule: namelist syntax where upstream writes Lisp), and `send_queued_mail_with_smtp` (smtpmail-send-queued-mail) sends the queue.
