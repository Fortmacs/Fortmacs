2026-11-02 — Terminal resizing and suspension (part of roadmap step 42e, sysdep.c): resizing the terminal now resizes the frame, and C-z suspends Emacs.  The SIGWINCH handler still only sets a flag (sysdep_mod's `deliver_window_change_signal`); a SIGWINCH now ends the wait for input, and the redisplay that follows runs dispnew.c's `do_pending_window_change`, which first does what upstream's handler does (`handle_window_change_signal`: read the terminal's size, request the change delayed) and then makes it (`change_frame_size`, `change_frame_size_1`, `frame_size_change_delayed`, and frame.c's `adjust_frame_size` with INHIBIT 5: the windows resized by window.c's `resize_frame_windows` down to window.el's `frame-windows-min-size` with IGNORE `safe`, the terminal's size set, the matrices reallocated, the costs recomputed, the frame garbaged).  keyboard.c's `suspend-emacs` (submodule keyboard_suspend_mod, with `stuff_buffered_input` over TIOCSTI) runs `suspend_hook`, gives the terminal back (sysdep.c's `reset_all_sys_modes` and the terminal part of `reset_sys_modes`, in term_mod: the last line cleared, full-screen mode left, the modes restored after tcdrain), stops the process with `sys_suspend` (SIGTSTP), takes the terminal again (`init_all_sys_modes`, the frame redrawn), resizes the frame if the terminal's size changed meanwhile, and runs `suspend_resume_hook`; frame.el's `suspend-frame` calls it on the controlling terminal and is bound to C-z and C-x C-z.  Tests: change-frame-size with one and two windows and the minimum size, window-change-signal on a pty, and suspend-emacs (in test_dispnew_mod, after the terminal tests, since setting up a terminal registers its function keys), which first moves the test process into a process group of its own and ignores SIGTSTP (it skips when the process already leads its group).  Deferred with reasons: `frame-size-history`, the window change hooks, `dont_resize_frames`, PRETEND, `cannot-suspend` and the subshell (GNU/Linux has job control, so upstream runs it only when that is set), the foreground process group (`narrow_foreground_group`), `suspend-tty` and `iconify-or-deiconify-frame`.

Follow-up (the owner's review): no approximation in resizing.  A split frame's windows now share a size change as upstream shares it: window.c's `resize_frame_windows` calls window.el's `window--resize-root-window` (not `window--resize-root-window-vertically`, which only the minibuffer window's resizing uses), ported in window_mod as `window_resize_root_window` with `window--resize-reset` (`window_resize_reset`), `window--resize-this-window` (`window_resize_this_window`), the proportional part of `window--resize-child-windows` (`window_resize_child_windows`: one line or column at a time to the window furthest from its normal size, never below its minimum size), `window-sizable` and `window-sizable-p` (`window_sizable`, `is_window_sizable`), `window-min-delta` and `window--min-delta-1` (`window_min_delta`, `window_min_delta_1`), `window-size` and `window-min-size` with IGNORE t, and window.c's `window_resize_check` and `window_resize_apply` (sizes in the new `new_total` component); `resize_frame_windows` tries with `window_min_height` and `window_min_width` first, then ignoring them.  Windows now keep normal sizes (`normal_lines`, `normal_cols`, window.c's `window-normal-size`), set as upstream sets them by split-window, delete-window, delete-other-windows, window-resize (with the next window) and window configurations, and copied by `replace_window`.  `window--resize-apply-p` and `Fwindow_resize_apply` are not needed: resize_frame_windows checks and applies directly, and window sizes are whole lines and columns, so `window--pixel-to-total` has nothing to round.  The change-frame-size/windows test now checks a 24-line frame split into windows of 10 and 13 lines resized to 30, 12, 10 (the upper window at `window_min_height`), 7 (below it) and 24 lines, giving 13/16, 5/6, 4/5, 3/3 and 10/13, then the upper window split into 30 and 50 columns resized to 100, 50, 33 and 20 columns, giving 37/63, 19/31, 12/21 and 10/10; the sizes are what GNU Emacs 29.3 (whose window.el resizing functions are identical to 31.1's) gives in a terminal resized to each size.
